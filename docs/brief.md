# Business brief

## What we sell
Custom LED **puddle lights**: door / side-mirror projectors that shine a logo, name or design on
the ground when the car door opens. US-facing direct-to-consumer store. Solo founder.
Brand name and domain are undecided: use `{{BRAND}}` (single source: `content/site.ts`).

### Product lines
1. **Vehicle-specific OEM-replacement lights** (main product): plug into the factory puddle-light
   connector. Sold per *housing* (many vehicles share one housing).
2. **Universal wireless lights**: cars without factory lights, bikes, boats, classics.

Glass-etched lenses only (never fade). Option axes:
- single vs dual lens
- door vs mirror location
- 2 or 4 lights
- light color
- custom vs pre-made design
- design-service add-on

## Customers (priority order)
1. **Personal gifts**: names, initials, weddings, first car.
2. **Businesses**: realtors, detailers, dealerships, fleets.
3. **Enthusiasts**: Tesla/EV, trucks, Dodge, Porsche.
4. **Later**: motorcycles, boats.

## Competitor
Main competitor: custompuddlelights.com (Ludicrous Revolution Inc, TX; also runs PimpMyEV).

Their weaknesses (our opportunities):
- Customers check fitment by emailing photos → we offer a one-tap visual housing picker.
- "Beta" vehicle search → we offer structured Year/Make/Model/Generation plus part number and VIN.
- Slow (1–6 weeks) → we show a clear "ships X days after proof approval" estimate and order stages.
- No live preview → we offer a live projection preview.
- No proof flow → we offer proof approval with 2 free revisions.
- No B2B portal → we offer a business quote and approval flow.

**NEVER copy their name, text, images, layout or reviews.**

## The three things that win
1. **Fitment certainty**: the customer confirms the exact light housing in one tap.
2. **Live projection preview**: upload a logo or type a name, then see it glowing on the ground at night.
3. **Rich but fast**: more animation and features than competitors, and still faster.

## Legal and trust rules
- No car-maker logos as designs (trademarks). Our pre-made library is original art only.
- Customer uploads require a rights checkbox ("I own or have permission to use this artwork").
- No fake reviews or stats. All sample reviews, gallery items and counts are labelled **Sample**.
- Placeholder media is clearly marked; never competitor images.
